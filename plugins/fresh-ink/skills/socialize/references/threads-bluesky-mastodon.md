# Threads, Bluesky, Mastodon

**Last checked:** November 2026. Tags: [platform] statement or code, [study] third-party data with a sample, [reported] single or vendor source.

Three conversational feeds that punish broadcast copy. Write like you're talking to people who might answer, because on all three the replies are the reach.

## Threads (Meta)
**Who's there:** a broad, casual audience; brands that do well post often and talk like people.
- **Limit:** 500 characters per post; a text attachment can add up to 10,000 more. One topic tag per post.
- **First line is a headline.** [platform] Threads posts now surface in Google Discover, so the opening line has to make sense to a stranger with no context.
- **Links:** allowed, but [platform] native link-click stats were removed (September 2026). Add UTM parameters to anything you need to measure.
- **Register:** conversational and quick. [reported] "Done beats perfect" is the house advice; a line that sounds like it came from a deck gets rewritten.
- **What works:** an opinion with a reason, a small observation, a question you actually want answered, replies to other people's posts.

## Bluesky
**Who's there:** skews technical, journalistic, and academic. Rewards nuance and receipts; hostile to hype and growth-hacker voice.
- **Limit:** 300 characters (the tightest of the three).
- **Replies rank by social proximity.** [platform] Community replies beat broadcast posts.
- **Threads:** each post should be a mini-argument with its own point, not one paragraph cut at arbitrary places.
- **Custom feeds** decide discovery for many users; specific keywords and topics help a post land in the right ones.
- **Tip:** a post written to fit Bluesky's 300 usually works on Threads and X too. Draft here first when posting to all three.

## Mastodon
**Who's there:** federated communities, often technical, with strong norms against marketing.
- **Limit:** 500 characters by default (some servers allow more).
- **No algorithmic feed.** Posts reach followers in order and spread by boosts. **Hashtags matter here** more than anywhere else: they're the main way strangers find a post. Use CamelCase (#OpenSource) so screen readers read them correctly.
- **Content warnings (CW)** are a norm for sensitive topics and sometimes for long posts or spoilers; follow the server's conventions.
- **Alt text** on every image is expected, not optional.
- Plain, useful, human. Brand accounts that post like ads get muted or defederated.

## Checklist
- [ ] Opening line works with zero context (Threads) or within 300 characters (Bluesky)
- [ ] Written to start a conversation, not to broadcast
- [ ] Mastodon: CamelCase hashtags, alt text, CW if appropriate
- [ ] UTM on any Threads link you need to measure
