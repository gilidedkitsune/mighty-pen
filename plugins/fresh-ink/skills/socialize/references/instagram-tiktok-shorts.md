# Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts

**Last checked:** November 2026. Tags: [platform] statement or code, [study] third-party data with a sample, [reported] single or vendor source.

Visual-first feeds. The writing job is the on-screen text, the spoken script, and the caption. Socialize drafts words and shot notes; it doesn't make the visuals.

## Originality rules (all three)
- [platform] Instagram's originality rules cover photos and carousels as well as video (April 2026): aggregator accounts lose recommendations. Commentary, remixes, voiceover, and collabs count as original; watermarks and crops don't.
- [platform] YouTube Shorts cuts reach for re-uploads (October 2026). Narration that only describes someone else's footage doesn't count as transformation. YouTube also demonetizes templated or mass-produced content, including generic AI templates without the creator's own insight.
- [platform] Realistic AI-generated visuals need the platform's AI label on TikTok and YouTube. TikTok says the label doesn't reduce organic reach.

Practical rule: scripts carry the creator's own analysis, example, or experience. Never draft a script that narrates footage the user doesn't own.

## Short-video scripts (Reels, TikTok, Shorts)
- **Length:** 15 to 30 seconds to start; up to 60 when every beat earns its place.
- **The first second:** three hooks at once: what's on screen, what's said, and the text overlay. Show the result or the problem first, never a logo or an intro.
- **Structure options:**
  - Problem to solution: problem (0 to 3 s), why it matters (3 to 10), the fix (10 to 25), what to do next (25 to 30).
  - Result first: show the end result, then "here's how," then quick steps, then the result again.
  - List: "3 things that..." with one item every 5 to 8 seconds.
- **Script format:** a two-column table, time and visual on the left, spoken line and on-screen text on the right.
- **Captions on:** most viewing is muted. Two lines on screen at most, 3 to 5 words per line.
- **Write for the send.** [reported] Instagram's leadership has named sends (shares by DM) as one of the strongest signals for Reels reach; aim for "I need to show someone this," not "nice."
- **Shorts:** the title is searchable, so use the words people search for. **TikTok:** put the key term in the first three words of the caption; captions are searched too.

## Captions
- **Instagram:** up to 2,200 characters; about 125 show before "more." Lead with the hook, not the hashtags. 3 to 5 hashtags at most (Instagram caps it at 5).
- **TikTok:** up to 4,000 characters, about 150 visible; often 5 to 10 words is enough. Up to 5 hashtags.
- **Shorts:** title up to 100 characters (about 70 visible). 1 to 5 hashtags; more than 15 and YouTube ignores all of them.

## Carousels (Instagram)
- 5 to 10 slides. Slide 1 opens a question or makes a promise; middle slides deliver one point each; the last slide is quotable or gives one next step.
- Pick the shape before writing slides: a value stack (tip after tip), problem to proof, a step-by-step walkthrough, or a before-and-after.
- Large type, one idea per slide, under about 25 words per slide.
- [study] Carousels' lead over single photos has shrunk, so use them for content that's genuinely sequential.

## Checklist
- [ ] The first second has a visual, a spoken, and a text hook
- [ ] The creator's own insight is in the script, not narration of borrowed footage
- [ ] Captions written for sound-off viewing
- [ ] Caption hook inside the visible characters; hashtags within each platform's cap
- [ ] AI label flagged if any visuals are realistic and AI-generated
