# Platform limits

**Last checked:** November 2026. Limits change; check the platform's help center when a post is close to one.

| Platform | Max length | Visible before "more" | Hashtags |
|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn post | 3,000 characters | ~210 characters | No cap; 0 to 3, at the end |
| LinkedIn comment | 1,250 characters | ~2 lines | Rarely |
| X post | 280 characters (Premium: 25,000) | Full post at 280; long posts truncate | 0 to 2 |
| Threads | 500 characters (+10,000 text attachment) | First 1 to 2 lines | 1 topic tag |
| Bluesky | 300 characters | Full post | Optional |
| Mastodon | 500 characters (varies by server) | Full post, unless behind a CW | Yes, CamelCase |
| Reddit title | 300 characters | Full title | No |
| Hacker News title | 80 characters | Full title | No |
| Instagram caption | 2,200 characters | ~125 characters | Max 5 |
| TikTok caption | 4,000 characters | ~150 characters | Max 5 |
| YouTube Shorts title | 100 characters | ~70 characters | 1 to 5 (over 15 and all are ignored) |
| Email subject | No hard limit | 30 to 50 characters on mobile | No |

Hashtags count toward the character limit everywhere.
